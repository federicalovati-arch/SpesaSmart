# **App Name**: SpesaIntelligente

## Core Features:

- Product & Price Catalog Management: Users can manually add, edit, and categorize products, recording their prices across different supermarkets. This forms the central database for all items.
- Supermarket Directory: Allow users to manage a list of their local or preferred supermarkets, with options to add, edit, or remove store information.
- Shopping List Creation: Enable users to create multiple shopping lists, adding specific products from their catalog and specifying quantities required for each item.
- Intelligent Price Comparison & Recommendation: For any given shopping list, the app will automatically calculate and display the lowest price for each item across recorded supermarkets. It will also compare total list costs for buying everything from a single store versus purchasing each item at its cheapest location.
- Flexible Shopping Path Optimization: Provide options to adjust shopping list recommendations based on user preferences, such as restricting purchases to a single specified supermarket, even if it's not the absolute cheapest overall.
- Shopping List Progress Tracking: Offer an intuitive interface within each shopping list to mark items as 'purchased' or 'in cart', providing a clear overview of shopping progress.
- AI Product Categorization Tool: A generative AI tool that automatically suggests categories or standardizes names for newly entered products, improving data organization and searchability within the catalog.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A modern and calm blue (#228CE6), reflecting clarity, efficiency, and intelligence.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated blue (#E6F2FA) to maintain a clean, airy feel that is easy on the eyes.
- Accent color: A vibrant, clear cyan (#19CCCC), used sparingly to highlight interactive elements, calls-to-action, and key information, providing a striking contrast against the primary palette.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a highly legible grotesque-style sans-serif, chosen for its modern, objective, and neutral aesthetic that prioritizes clarity and efficiency for data display and textual content.
- Utilize a consistent set of line-art icons that are simple and clear, depicting concepts such as shopping carts, price tags, checkmarks, product categories, and store locations. This reinforces the app's utility and modern appeal.
- Implement a clean and structured layout with clear content segmentation, utilizing whitespace effectively to enhance readability of lists and comparison tables. The design will be responsive to ensure usability across various device sizes.
- Incorporate subtle and swift animations for transitions between sections, adding or removing list items, and displaying interactive feedback. Animations will be purposeful, aiming to enhance the user experience without being distracting.
